The question behind the work

How do we make
clinical AI useful?

A good prediction is a starting point. I’m interested in whether a model works in a new health system, captures changes over time, and offers information clinicians can interpret.

At Houston Methodist, I work with Sadeer Al-Kindi and Khurram Nasir on these questions, bringing machine learning methods into close conversation with cardiovascular medicine.
